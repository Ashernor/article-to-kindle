Article → Kindle

Send the web article you're reading to your Kindle, as a clean EPUB.

v1.0 · open-source (MIT)

Step 1 · Enable the extension

Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions, turn on Article → Kindle, then allow it on the sites you read.

You can turn on Article to Kindle’s extension in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

Article to Kindle’s extension is currently on. You can turn it off in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

Article to Kindle’s extension is currently off. You can turn it on in the Extensions section of Safari Settings.

Step 2 · Use it

  1. Open any article in Safari.
  2. Tap the Article → Kindle button in the toolbar (or via the page menu).
  3. Tap Send to Kindle — download the EPUB, or email it to your Kindle.

Delivery settings

Pick Download or Email, and set your Kindle address and relay, inside the extension: open it from Safari and tap Settings. Email delivery needs a one-time, free setup:
