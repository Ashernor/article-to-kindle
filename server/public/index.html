Article → Kindle

Send the web article you're reading to your Kindle, as a clean EPUB.

Download for iPhone & iPad Add to Chrome GitHub

Why

  • EPUB, not PDF. Kindles reflow EPUB the way e-ink is meant to be read — PDFs don't.
  • Clean reading. Strips nav, ads and clutter with Mozilla Readability, keeps the text and images.
  • Private by design. The article is converted on your device. Optional email delivery goes through a relay you host — nothing goes to us.
  • Free & open-source (MIT). Build it yourself, or support development with an optional tip.

How it works

  • Open an article and click the toolbar icon (or right-click → Send to Kindle).
  • The article is extracted and turned into a clean EPUB, right in your browser.
  • Download the .epub, or one-click email it to your @kindle.com address via your own relay.
