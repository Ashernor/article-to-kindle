Privacy Policy — Article → Kindle

Last updated: 2026-08-23

Article → Kindle is private by default. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party.

What it does

  • Article extraction and EPUB conversion happen entirely on your device. In Download mode, nothing leaves your browser.
  • Settings (delivery mode, Kindle address, relay URL, optional token) are stored locally in the browser's extension storage and never sent to the developer.
  • Email mode sends the EPUB and your Kindle address to a relay server you deploy and control, which emails it via your own mailbox. The developer never receives your content or credentials.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no account, no developer-operated backend.

Optional in-app tips

Tips in the iOS/macOS app are processed by Apple under Apple's terms; we never see your payment details.
