# Privacy Policy — Article → Kindle

_Last updated: 2026-08-23_

Article → Kindle is designed to be private by default. It does **not** collect,
transmit, sell, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party.

## What the app/extension does

- **Article extraction and EPUB conversion happen entirely on your device.** In
  *Download* mode, nothing ever leaves your browser.
- **Settings** (delivery mode, your Kindle address, your relay URL, an optional
  token) are stored locally using the browser's extension storage. If your
  browser syncs extension data across your own devices, those settings sync with
  it. They are never sent to the developer.
- **Email mode** sends the generated EPUB and your Kindle address **to a relay
  server that you deploy and control** (e.g. your own Netlify site), which then
  emails it through your own mailbox. The developer does not operate this relay
  and never receives your content, your Kindle address, or your credentials.

## What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no account, and no
developer-operated backend that receives your data.

## Optional in-app tips

If you choose to leave a tip in the iOS/macOS app, the purchase is processed by
Apple under Apple's terms. We receive only the anonymized payout information
Apple provides; we never see your payment details.
